# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels. Keep discussions respectful, accessible and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, hardware evidence and decisions rather than attacking people.
- Give constructive feedback and accept respectful disagreement.
- Respect contributors' time, privacy and language preferences.
- Be honest about authorship, AI assistance, test results and limitations.
- Credit prior work and respect its license.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, impersonation, disclosure of private information without permission and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and reporting

These expectations apply to participation in this repository, including issues, PRs and reviews. They do not restrict independent use or modification under MIT.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer if a private channel is available. Otherwise, a public issue may request a private contact method without publishing sensitive details or identifying affected people. GitHub's reporting tools are also available for platform violations.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, restrict participation or report violations to GitHub. No response time or confidentiality guarantee is promised.
